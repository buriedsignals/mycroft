## claude-skills-journalism (MIT)

Four journalism skills — `foia-requests`, `interview-prep`, `story-pitch`,
and `ai-writing-detox` — are adapted from
[claude-skills-journalism](https://github.com/jamditis/claude-skills-journalism)
by **Joe Amditis** (Center for Cooperative Media, Montclair State University),
MIT License — vendored at `2097d218` with localization and integration edits
for Mycroft. Each skill carries its own attribution frontmatter and Credits
footer; re-vendoring is managed by the engine's `sync-upstream-skills` tool
against the pinned upstream commit recorded in the skill catalog.

## Website media and dependencies

Tool clips and partner interface images have provenance records in
[`assets/previews/README.md`](assets/previews/README.md) and
[`assets/screenshots/README.md`](assets/screenshots/README.md).
